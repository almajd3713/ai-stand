How does a picture hide you?

  1. The AI (YOLO) finds people by adding up thousands of tiny visual clues: edges, textures, shapes.
  2. We froze the AI and let a computer redraw the patch thousands of times, keeping each change that made the AI less sure it saw a person.
  3. The result looks like nonsense to you, but it drowns out the clues the AI relies on.
